# Seasonal Agriculture Performance Data Analytics

## Project Overview

This project analyzes agricultural performance across different seasons, crops, regions, environmental conditions, resource usage, and economic outcomes.

The analysis focuses on identifying seasonal patterns, crop performance differences, resource efficiency, relationships between environmental factors and agricultural outcomes, and regional consistency.

## Objectives

- Explore and understand the agricultural dataset
- Clean and prepare the data for analysis
- Compare agricultural performance across seasons
- Analyze crop-wise and regional performance
- Examine environmental factors affecting agricultural outcomes
- Study irrigation, fertilizer, and water usage
- Analyze profitability and economic performance
- Identify meaningful patterns and unusual observations
- Develop data-driven recommendations for agricultural planning

## Dataset

The dataset contains **4,000 records and 28 variables** covering:

- Crop and season information
- State and district
- Farm area
- Rainfall and temperature
- Humidity and sunlight
- Soil conditions
- NPK nutrients
- Irrigation methods
- Fertilizer and pesticide usage
- Seed quality
- Yield and production
- Market price
- Cost, revenue, and profit
- Water usage and water efficiency
- Disease and pest risk

## Key Findings

- Kharif shows the strongest overall seasonal performance in the dataset.
- Sugarcane is a major high-performing crop in terms of yield and profitability.
- Zaid shows comparatively weaker economic performance.
- Drip irrigation combined with higher nutrient intensity shows strong economic return per unit of water.
- Agricultural performance varies across regions and seasons.
- Environmental conditions show identifiable ranges associated with higher yields.
